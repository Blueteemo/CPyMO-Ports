Derivative modifications: Unicode coverage subset for CPyMO, preservation of
the previously validated handheld font's vertical metrics and layout policy,
and derivative font-family/PostScript names changed to CPyMO CJK Medium /
CPyMOCJK-Medium to respect the reserved font name.

7,200 codepoints, including every codepoint in the prior 7,170-codepoint font.
The original glyph bitmaps/advances and ASCII kerning were compared using
CPyMO's actual stb_truetype rasterizer; outlines and weight are unchanged.
